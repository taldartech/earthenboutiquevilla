ABOUT US

Nestled in the picturesque outskirts of Udaipur, our Earthen Boutique Villa is a harmonious blend of rustic charm and modern luxury. Here, amid the serene countryside, we invite you to embark on a unique and immersive journey that celebrates the rich cultural heritage of Rajasthan.Our boutique villa is a labor of love, inspired by the timeless traditions of Rajasthan's earthen architecture. Our villa seamlessly blends into its natural surroundings, creating an atmosphere of tranquility and authenticity.

Experience the essence of Rajasthan in our thoughtfully designed villas and suites. Each abode is a testament to craftsmanship and attention to detail, featuring traditional earthen architecture complemented by modern amenities & plunge pool. Wake up to the soothing sounds of nature and the scent of blooming flowers right outside your window.
